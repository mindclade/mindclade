# Code of Conduct

Mindclade contributors must maintain a professional, safe, and inclusive
working environment. Treat colleagues with respect, critique ideas and evidence
rather than people, and make room for differing backgrounds and expertise.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate humiliation, retaliation,
unwanted sexual attention, disclosure of private information, and knowingly
unsafe or deceptive engineering conduct are unacceptable in repository spaces,
reviews, incidents, research discussions, and related events.

Raise concerns privately with the repository maintainers or the appropriate
security, people, or biological-safety owner. Maintainers must limit disclosure,
avoid conflicts of interest, preserve necessary evidence, and respond
proportionately. Responses may include correction, warning, temporary access
restriction, removal from the project, or escalation under company policy and
law. Good-faith reporting is protected from retaliation.
